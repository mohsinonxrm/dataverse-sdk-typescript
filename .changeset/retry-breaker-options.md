Retry backoff and jitter options, and a failure-ratio circuit breaker (#476). New options only; the defaults are unchanged.

- **Retry:** `backoff` (`"exponential"`, the default, `"linear"` or `"constant"`); `jitter: "decorrelated"` for Polly v8's decorrelated jitter (`DecorrelatedJitterBackoffV2`), capped at `maxDelayMs` (`true`, ±20%, stays the default); `retryAfterJitterMs` (default 0) adds up to that many ms to each `Retry-After` wait, shrunk so the wait still ends before the request's deadline; `shouldRetry` gets a third argument, `{ request, response, attempt }` (one- and two-parameter predicates work as before, and the idempotency guard still applies after it); `retryAttemptTimeouts` (default `false`) retries an attempt that ran out of `attemptTimeoutMs`, for idempotent requests only (a POST or a PATCH without `If-Match` also needs `retryNonIdempotent: true`), and never past the deadline.
- **Circuit breaker:** `mode: "ratio"` opens the circuit when, within `samplingDurationMs` (default 30 s), at least `minimumThroughput` (default 10) requests succeeded or failed and at least `failureRatio` (default 0.5) of them failed; neutral outcomes (429, other 4xx) count neither way. `breakDurationMs` can be a function of `{ failures, consecutiveOpens }`. `isolate()` holds the circuit open until `reset()` (or the new `close()`), with `isolated` to read it. `DataverseCircuitOpenError.retryAfterMs` is now also set while a probe is in flight: the rest of a break duration counted from when the probe started, after which the next request may probe too. `now` injects the clock for tests. State changes go to the client's telemetry as `dataverse.circuit.state` (`TelemetryEvents.circuitState`), with the state (`closed`, `open`, `half-open` or `isolated`) and the reason.
